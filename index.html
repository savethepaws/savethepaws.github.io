Social Media Marketing Plan

Buyer Personas

Course requirement: 2–3 buyer personas representing the people Save the Paws needs to reach — adopters, fosters, volunteers, and donors.

É

Émilie Bouchard

Prospective Cat or Dog Adopter
Age29
LocationSt-Léonard, Montreal, QC
OccupationMarketing Coordinator (hybrid)
Income~$58,000 CAD
EducationBachelor's degree
HouseholdLives alone, pet-friendly rental apartment
Who she is

Has wanted a pet since moving into her own apartment two years ago. Now that her schedule and finances are stable, she's actively looking to adopt rather than buy, because she believes rescue animals deserve a real chance at a home. She researches carefully and gets attached to specific animals' stories before ever filling out an application. She's compassionate but cautious — she values animal welfare, honesty and transparency, ethical adoption over buying from breeders, supporting local causes, and long-term commitment, and won't commit until she's confident the animal is a good match.

Goals
  • Find a healthy cat or dog whose temperament fits her lifestyle and apartment.
  • Give a rescued animal a safe, permanent home.
  • Understand the animal's health, personality, and care needs before committing.
  • Adopt from a trustworthy Quebec rescue organization and feel supported throughout.
Fears & pain points
  • Adopting an animal whose energy or personality doesn't fit her apartment or lifestyle.
  • Discovering an unexpected health or behavioural issue, or underestimating ongoing vet/food/care costs.
  • Not receiving enough information about the animal's history, or facing a slow, unclear application process.
What Save the Paws can realistically offer
  • Detailed online pet profiles with available veterinary records and honest behavioural notes from volunteers/fosters.
  • A lifestyle-based application, a scheduled meet-and-greet, and transparent adoption-fee information.
  • An adoption-prep checklist and a post-adoption follow-up, with referrals to vets or trainers when appropriate.
Customer journey

Awareness (sees a rescue story or adoptable-animal post on social) → Interest (browses available animals) → Consideration (reads health & personality info) → Trust (testimonials, comments, questions) → Action (applies, attends meet-and-greet) → Post-adoption (transition guidance & follow-up check-in).
